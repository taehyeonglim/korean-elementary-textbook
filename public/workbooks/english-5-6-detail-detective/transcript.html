Where: School library

Bring: one favorite book

Meet Ms. Han by the front desk.

  3. 장소를 쓰세요. 정답: school library
  4. 가져갈 것을 쓰세요. 정답: one favorite book
  5. 만날 사람을 쓰세요. 정답: Ms. Han

  1. When is the club? 정답: Friday at 3:30 p.m.
  2. Where do students meet Ms. Han? 정답: by the front desk
